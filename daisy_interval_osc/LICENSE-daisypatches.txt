Licence for the IntervalOsc sources in this directory
=====================================================

This directory is Nick Donaldson's IntervalOsc patch, from
https://github.com/ndonald2/DaisyPatches/tree/main/patches/IntervalOsc
(itself a soft fork of Electrosmith's DaisyExamples, carrying its MIT licence).

    IntervalOsc.cpp   his patch source
    README.md         his README, kept as written

intervalosc_voice.h and intervalosc_voice.cpp are a port of that DSP onto the
daisy_multiosc universal panel: the oscillator behaviour, the interval/rational
modes and the ratio table are his; the engine wrapper, panel mapping and
soft-takeover are new work by David Baghurst. Both copyrights are noted in those
files and both apply.
